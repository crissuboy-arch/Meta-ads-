# Video Ads Structure

This skill enables the user to architect high-performance video ad structures for Meta Ads, Google Ads, and TikTok Ads, focusing on the sales funnel and creative optimization.

---

## Keywords

Video ad structure, Ad hooks, Ad storytelling, Effective CTA, Meta Ads Video, Google Ads Performance Max, TikTok Spark Ads, VSL (Video Sales Letter), CPV, Optimized ROAS, Video retention, AIDA for video, Performance editing, Ad captions.

---

## Quick Start

1.  **Start with a "Pattern Interrupt" (0-3s)**: Use a shocking/curious visual or sound that interrupts the user's scroll, like "Is your business losing X% of customers to the competition?" or an unexpected, vibrant scene.
2.  **Present the Problem and Pain (3-10s)**: Quickly connect with the audience, illustrating a common frustration or challenge your product/service solves. Show the problem before presenting the solution.
3.  **Demonstrate the Solution in Action (10-25s)**: Show the product or service solving the problem clearly and attractively, focusing on practical benefits and results.
4.  **End with a Strong, Visually Impactful CTA (25-30s)**: Make sure the call to action is explicit, with a clear action verb ("Shop Now," "Book Your Demo," "Download the Ebook") and the button or link easily visible.

## Core Workflows

### Workflow 1: Top-of-Funnel Video Ad Structure Creation (Awareness/Engagement)

This workflow focuses on capturing attention on fast-consumption platforms like TikTok and Meta Ads (Reels, Stories), aiming for brand recognition and initial engagement with optimized CPV.

1.  **Objective and Platform Definition**:
    *   **Objective**: Generate brand recognition and high-quality video views.
    *   **Platforms**: TikTok Ads (Spark Ads, In-Feed), Meta Ads (Reels, Stories, Feed).
    *   **Format**: Vertical (9:16), 15-30 seconds long.
2.  **Interruption Hook Construction (0-5s)**:
    *   **Example**: For a personal organizer service: "Does your house look like chaos? 🤯 See how I organized this apartment in just 2 hours!" (Visual: Quick scene of a messy bedroom, followed by a close-up of the smiling personal organizer).
    *   **Rule**: Must be visually dynamic, with on-screen text and, if possible, an attention-grabbing sound.
3.  **Problem and Desire Presentation (5-10s)**:
    *   **Example**: "End the clutter stress and gain time for what really matters." (Visual: Frustrated person looking for something, transition to a relaxed person in an organized space).
    *   **Rule**: Connect the audience's pain with the desire to overcome it, using captions for those watching without audio.
4.  **Solution Introduction (10-20s)**:
    *   **Example**: "With the 'Organize & Live' method, we transform your space and your routine." (Visual: Quick before/after slides of organized spaces, showing the organization process dynamically).
    *   **Rule**: Show the "how" in an engaging way, without going too deep into details, but enough to spark curiosity.
5.  **Discovery Call (20-30s)**:
    *   **Example**: "Ready for a lighter, more organized life? Tap to learn more about our plans!" (Visual: Animated "LEARN MORE" button with the brand logo. Motivational music).
    *   **Rule**: Soft CTA, pointing to an informative landing page or social profile, focused on "learn more" or "explore."
6.  **Focus Metrics**:
    *   **CPV**: Target $0.02 - $0.05.
    *   **Retention Rate (3s)**: > 45%.
    *   **Completion Rate**: > 20%.

### Workflow 2: Bottom-of-Funnel Video Ad Structure Optimization (Conversion/Sales)

This workflow optimizes the video structure to generate direct sales or qualified leads, using social proof and a sense of urgency, ideal for Google Ads (Performance Max, YouTube In-Stream) and Meta Ads (Feed, Stories).

1.  **Objective and Platform Definition**:
    *   **Objective**: Generate direct sales (ecommerce) or qualified leads (B2B services/courses).
    *   **Platforms**: Google Ads (Performance Max, YouTube In-Stream), Meta Ads (Feed, Stories).
    *   **Format**: Horizontal (16:9) or Vertical (9:16) depending on placement, 30-60 seconds long.
2.  **Direct Problem/Solution Hook (0-8s)**:
    *   **Example**: For financial management software: "Is managing your finances a nightmare? Discover how to save 10h/month and increase your profit by 20% with [Software Name]!" (Visual: Stressed person with spreadsheets, transition to a rising profit chart).
    *   **Rule**: Address the problem and promise a measurable benefit right at the start.
3.  **Social Proof and Credibility (8-20s)**:
    *   **Example**: "Companies like [Company X] and [Company Y] have already optimized their results. See what the CEO of [Company Z] says: 'Our ROI increased 3x since we implemented [Software Name]!'" (Visual: Short video testimonial, client logos, award badges).
    *   **Rule**: Use real testimonials, success numbers, or relevant client logos to build trust.
4.  **Key Benefit Demonstration (20-40s)**:
    *   **Example**: "With [Software Name], you get: Intuitive dashboard, real-time reports, payment automation, and bank integration." (Visual: Software interface in use, highlighting each feature and its direct benefit).
    *   **Rule**: Focus on tangible benefits like "save time," "reduce costs," "increase productivity," with clear visuals.
5.  **Offer and Sense of Urgency/Scarcity (40-50s)**:
    *   **Example**: "Get 30 days free plus 10% off your first annual plan! Offer valid for the next 7 days only!" (Visual: Countdown timer, crossed-out price, promotional price, "LAST SPOTS" text).
    *   **Rule**: Create a sense of urgency to encourage immediate action.
6.  **Explicit Final Call to Action (50-60s)**:
    *   **Example**: "Don't waste any more time! Click the link and start your financial transformation today! [Button: GET MY DISCOUNT NOW!]" (Visual: Large flashing CTA button, website URL highlighted).
    *   **Rule**: Direct CTA with clear instructions, reinforcing the offer and benefit.
7.  **Focus Metrics**:
    *   **ROAS**: Target > 3.0.
    *   **CPA**: Target < $50.
    *   **Conversion Rate**: > 2%.

## Checklist

- [x] Define a hook in the first 3 seconds that interrupts the scroll and creates curiosity.
- [x] Present the target audience's problem before 10 seconds of video, generating connection.
- [x] Demonstrate the solution (product/service) in use, focusing on visual benefits and practical results.
- [x] Include social proof (testimonial, success numbers, credibility badges) to generate trust.
- [x] Create a single, clear CTA with an explicit sense of urgency or scarcity.
- [x] Optimize the video for sound-off viewing (clear captions, on-screen text, self-explanatory infographics).
- [x] Test multiple creatives (A/B testing) with variations of hooks, CTAs, and problem/solution structures.
- [x] Ensure the video's pace and duration fit the platform and objective (short for awareness, medium for conversion).
- [x] Insert the brand/logo in the first seconds and at the end of the video to reinforce branding.
- [x] Use soundtrack and sound effects that reinforce the message and maintain engagement.

## Common Mistakes

1.  **Excessive focus on the product and not the customer's problem/solution**: Many ads start by describing product features, losing the viewer's interest.
    *   **How to avoid it**: Start the video with a question or statement that resonates with the audience's pain or desire. E.g.: Instead of "Our car has a V8 engine and leather seats," use "Tired of traffic? See how our electric car gives you freedom in the city!"
2.  **Vague, multiple, or nonexistent call to action (CTA)**: Without a clear instruction, the user is lost on what action to take.
    *   **How to avoid it**: Have only ONE main CTA, clear and explicit, at the end of the video. E.g.: Instead of a generic "Learn More," use "Click and Get 20% OFF NOW!" or "Schedule your free demo."
3.  **Videos not optimized for sound-off consumption**: Most users watch social media videos with the sound off initially.
    *   **How to avoid it**: Use dynamic captions, on-screen text, and visual elements that convey the core message even without audio. E.g.: Add automatically generated captions or create animated text to highlight key points.
