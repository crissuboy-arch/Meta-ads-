# Seasonal Ads Calendar

This skill enables you to plan, execute, and optimize seasonal digital ad campaigns on Meta Ads, Google Ads, and TikTok Ads, maximizing ROAS and performance on specific commercial dates.

---

## Keywords

Black Friday Ads, Digital Christmas, Cyber Monday Google Ads, Easter Meta Ads, Mother's Day TikTok Ads, Seasonal performance campaigns, Event CPA optimization, Ecommerce ads calendar, Seasonal ROAS, Holiday ads, Seasonal Meta Ads, Google Ads events.

---

## Quick Start

1.  **Map Key Events:** List the 3 main seasonal dates for your ecommerce store in the next 6 months (e.g.: Black Friday, Christmas, Mother's Day).
2.  **Define Budget and ROAS:** For each date, set a total media budget (e.g.: $15,000 for Black Friday) and a Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) target (e.g.: 4.0x).
3.  **Configure Meta Ads:** On Meta Ads, create a "Sales" campaign for Black Friday, with "Interested in Electronics" and "Website Visitors in the Last 30 Days" audiences.
4.  **Configure Google Ads:** On Google Ads, set up a Performance Max campaign focused on "Sales" for the pre-Christmas period, using an updated product feed with a 4.5x target ROAS.
5.  **Configure TikTok Ads:** On TikTok Ads, set up a "Sales" campaign for Valentine's Day, targeting "Fashion and Relationship Interests," with short, dynamic videos.

## Core Workflows

### Workflow 1: Multiplatform Seasonal Campaign Strategic Planning

This workflow details the process of mapping dates, allocating budget, and defining platform-specific objectives for seasonal events, aiming to maximize performance.

1.  **Date Mapping and Prioritization:**
    *   **Identification:** List the seasonal dates relevant to the client's niche, such as Black Friday (November), Christmas (December), Presidents' Day (February), Mother's Day (May), Valentine's Day (February), Cyber Monday (November), Easter (March/April), and back-to-school (August).
    *   **Prioritization:** For an electronics ecommerce store, the most relevant dates would be Black Friday, Christmas, and Cyber Monday. For a personalized gifts store, Mother's Day, Valentine's Day, and Christmas would have higher priority. Prioritize the 3-5 most impactful.
    *   **Example:** For a women's fashion client, the primary dates would be Mother's Day, Valentine's Day, Black Friday, and Christmas.

2.  **Budget Allocation by Event and Platform:**
    *   **Annual Distribution:** Distribute the total annual media budget (e.g.: $300,000) considering seasonal peaks. Q4 (Black Friday/Christmas) can consume 40-50% of the total annual marketing budget.
    *   **Detailed Example:** For a $50,000 Q4 budget, allocate $25,000 to Black Friday (50%), $20,000 to Christmas (40%), and $5,000 to Cyber Monday (10%). Within the Black Friday budget ($25,000), distribute: $12,000 to Meta Ads (50%), $10,000 to Google Ads (40%), and $3,000 to TikTok Ads (10%).

3.  **Objective and Strategy Definition by Platform:**
    *   **Meta Ads (Facebook/Instagram):**
        *   **Objective:** "Sales" (conversion to "Purchases") or "Lead Generation."
        *   **Strategy:** For Black Friday, a "Sales" objective optimizing for "Purchases" is ideal. Use "Brand Awareness" or "Traffic" campaigns in the 2-3 weeks leading up to the event to warm up the audience and build anticipation.
        *   **Audiences:** Custom audiences of website visitors, cart adders, and 1%-5% Lookalikes of buyers or people who engaged with previous promotions.
    *   **Google Ads:**
        *   **Objective:** "Sales" (ecommerce or leads).
        *   **Strategy:** Use Performance Max with an updated product feed for ecommerce, setting an aggressive target ROAS (e.g.: 4.0x - 5.0x). For services, Search campaigns with manual bids or "Maximize Conversions" for high-intent, long-tail terms related to the event.
        *   **Example:** Performance Max campaign focused on "Christmas Electronics" with a 4.5x target ROAS, using themed asset groups (e.g.: "Tech Gifts," "Holiday Deals").
    *   **TikTok Ads:**
        *   **Objective:** "Sales" (Website Conversions) or "Lead Generation."
        *   **Strategy:** "Sales" campaigns focused on direct offers for younger, engaged audiences, using short, creative videos that generate a sense of urgency.
        *   **Example:** For Valentine's Day, a "Sales" campaign with UGC (User-Generated Content) creative videos showing couples interacting with the products, optimizing for "Purchases."

### Workflow 2: Seasonal Ad Execution and Dynamic Optimization

This workflow covers campaign implementation and real-time adjustments during seasonal periods, from pre-launch to post-event.

1.  **Pre-launch Phase (2-3 weeks before the event):**
    *   **Meta Ads:**
        *   Create "Brand Awareness" or "Traffic" campaigns with promotion teasers. E.g.: "Get ready for Black Friday! Unmissable discounts coming on 11/25."
        *   Develop creatives with countdowns or "coming soon."
        *   Build engagement custom audiences from these teasers for later remarketing.
    *   **Google Ads:**
        *   Update product feeds in Google Merchant Center with "Black Friday," "Christmas," or "Promotion" labels.
        *   Create specific Search ad groups with long-tail keywords and seasonal buying intent. E.g.: "Samsung phone Black Friday 2024," "Christmas gift for her."
        *   Prepare Performance Max campaigns with event-related text and visual assets.
    *   **TikTok Ads:**
        *   Launch reach or video view campaigns with fun, mysterious content, generating hype for the promotion.
        *   Consider hashtag challenges or influencer partnerships to create pre-event organic content.

2.  **Event Phase (Peak week and days):**
    *   **Meta Ads:**
        *   Shift focus to "Sales" campaigns with "CBO Budget (Campaign Budget Optimization)" enabled, distributing the budget among ad sets.
        *   **Targeting:** Use ad sets for remarketing (cart abandoners, product page visitors in the last 3 days) and buyer lookalikes (1% and 2%).
        *   **Example:** $1,000/day CBO budget, with 3 ad sets: Cart Abandoner Remarketing (high priority), 1% Buyer Lookalike, Deal Interests (cold audience).
        *   **Creatives:** Urgency and scarcity messages ("Last units!", "Today only!") and clear discount highlights.
    *   **Google Ads:**
        *   Increase bids for high-performing keywords and seasonal terms, monitoring "Search Impression Share" to ensure visibility.
        *   Adjust daily budgets to capture growing demand without breaking the limit.
        *   Use bid strategies like "Maximize conversion value" with target ROAS, lowering the target ROAS during peaks to maximize sales volume.
    *   **TikTok Ads:**
        *   Increase budget on "Sales" campaigns with "Website Conversions" focused on direct offers.
        *   Use creatives that generate a sense of urgency, showing products in use and the immediate purchase benefit.
        *   Explore Live Shopping for real-time sales during the event peak.

3.  **Post-Event Phase (1 week after):**
    *   **Meta Ads:**
        *   Run remarketing campaigns for those who viewed products or added to cart but didn't buy, focusing on "last chance" or "next offer." E.g.: "Don't miss out! Last hours of Black Friday discounts, grab yours now!"
        *   Point to complementary products or new arrivals.
    *   **Google Ads:**
        *   Reduce bids and budgets for seasonal terms.
        *   Focus on evergreen campaigns and dynamic remarketing for those who interacted with the seasonal promotion.
    *   **TikTok Ads:**
        *   Analyze campaign performance, collecting insights on creatives, audiences, and offers that performed best.
        *   Use this data to plan the next seasonal event.

### Seasonal Campaign Structure (Meta Ads - Black Friday)

```
Platform: Meta Ads (Facebook/Instagram)
Campaign Objective: Sales (Optimize for Purchases)
Campaign Name: BF2024_Sales_FullFunnel_Strategy
Period: 11/20/2024 - 11/29/2024 (11/20-11/24 Pre-BF, 11/25-11/29 BF Week)
Budget: $20,000 (Campaign Budget - CBO)
Bid Strategy: Lowest Cost (with bid cap for A/B tests)
Optimization Actions:
  - Set a daily spend cap on the campaign (e.g.: $2,500/day).
  - Monitor ROAS and CPA in real time, adjusting Ad Set budgets if necessary (even with CBO).

Ad Sets:

  - Ad Set 1: Remarketing - Cart Abandoners (last 7 days)
    - Name: BF2024_RMK_Cart7D
    - Budget: High Priority (E.g.: CBO will give ~$7,000)
    - Audience: Custom Audience of people who "Added to Cart" but didn't "Purchase" in the last 7 days.
    - Creatives: Dynamic carousel with abandoned cart products + CTA "Complete your purchase with [X]% OFF!"
    - Message: Urgency, scarcity, bonus to complete the purchase.

  - Ad Set 2: 1% Buyer Lookalike (last 180 days)
    - Name: BF2024_LLA1%_Buyers180D
    - Budget: Medium (E.g.: CBO will give ~$8,000)
    - Audience: 1% Lookalike of Custom Audience of "Buyers" in the last 180 days (base of 1,000+ buyers).
    - Creatives: Short unboxing videos of products on sale + CTA "Shop now! Black Friday discounts!"
    - Message: Highlight the main products on offer, social proof.

  - Ad Set 3: Broad Interests - Electronics, Fashion, Home & Decor (interest expansion)
    - Name: BF2024_Broad_Interests
    - Budget: Medium (E.g.: CBO will give ~$5,000)
    - Audience: Interests: "Black Friday," "Deals," "Online Shopping," "Electronics," "Fashion," "Home Decor" (with detailed targeting expansion on).
    - Creatives: Static images of specific offers with original price, discounted price, and discount percentage.
    - Message: Direct appeal to the promotion and the discount benefit.

Ads: 3-5 variations per Ad Set, testing formats (image, video, carousel) and messages (urgency, benefit, scarcity, social proof).
```
