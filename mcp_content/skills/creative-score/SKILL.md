# Creative Quality Checklist

Important: this is a **best-practices and compliance** checklist, not a performance forecast. No tool knows an ad's real CTR before it runs — the audience decides that. Make this explicit to the user whenever the checklist is presented.

## Mandatory checklist (each item: pass / fix)

**Hook (first words of the text and headline)**
- [ ] Uses a real term or pain from the research (not generic)
- [ ] Understandable in under 2 seconds of reading

**Offer clarity**
- [ ] It's clear what the product/service is and who it's for
- [ ] It's clear what the person gets by clicking (not just a vague "learn more")

**CTA**
- [ ] There is 1 and only 1 clear call to action
- [ ] The chosen button matches the real expected action on the landing page

**Proof/trust (when applicable)**
- [ ] Every social proof cited is real and verifiable (review, number, credential)
- [ ] No testimonial or number was invented

**Meta policy rejection risk**
- [ ] No individual-result promises (health, money, aesthetics, employment)
- [ ] No misleading before/after or exaggerated comparison
- [ ] No claims about the user's personal attributes ("you have [condition]," "you're overweight") — Meta prohibits targeting/copy that presumes a sensitive attribute of the individual
- [ ] If it's a regulated category (health, financial, employment, housing, politics), neutral language with no guarantees

**Format**
- [ ] Headline within the recommended limit (~40 characters) without cutting the main idea
- [ ] Primary text carries the essential info in the first ~125 characters (before the "See more" cut)
- [ ] The described visual suggestion is compatible with both feed AND Stories/Reels (vertical ratio prioritized, since that's where most current inventory is)

## Result

- **Approved**: passed every mandatory policy-risk item + at least 80% of the rest → moves on to `/campaign`.
- **Needs fixes**: failed some item — list the specific item and rewrite only the necessary part (don't discard the whole variation), then run the checklist again.
- Never mark as approved just because the user is in a hurry to publish — the checklist exists precisely to avoid launching ads with rejection risk or that make a bad impression for the brand.
