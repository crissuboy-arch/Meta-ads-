Privacy Policy

Meta Ads Expert PRO — Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. Data we collect

We collect only the data needed to operate the service:

  • Account data: your email address when you create an account.
  • Meta advertising data: when you connect your Meta ad accounts, we read campaign, ad set, ad and insights data through the official Meta API, only after you explicitly authorize the connection.
  • Usage data: basic technical logs (pages visited, errors) to keep the service running.

2. How we use your data

Your data is used solely to provide the service: generating ad strategies, displaying your campaign results, and tracking conversions. We never sell your data and never share it with third parties for marketing.

3. Meta API data

Data obtained through Meta APIs (ad accounts, campaigns, insights) is used only inside the app, at your request, to manage and analyze your own advertising. We do not transfer Meta data to any third party.

4. Data retention

We keep your data while your account is active. You may request deletion at any time (see Data Deletion).

5. Security

Connections use encrypted transport (HTTPS). API tokens you provide are stored server-side and never exposed in the browser.
