Data Deletion Instructions

Meta Ads Expert PRO — Last updated: October 6, 2026

How to request deletion of your data

If you want us to delete your personal data, follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to cristiane.fso@hotmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request".
  2. Use the same email address you registered with, so we can verify your identity.
  3. We will delete your account data, connected ad-account tokens and stored campaign data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email and login credentials).
  • Meta API tokens and connected ad-account references.
  • Saved drafts, tracking configurations and chat history.

What is kept

Anonymized billing records required by law (via Hotmart) are kept for the legally required period and contain no advertising data.

Meta data

Revoking the app's access can also be done directly in your Facebook settings: Settings → Apps and Websites → remove "Ofensiva Ads MCP".
